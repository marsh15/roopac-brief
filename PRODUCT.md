# Roopac Brief

Internal tool for ROOPAC, a custom print & packaging company in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu.
Turns messy customer enquiries (WhatsApp forwards, calls, emails) into grounded internal
job briefs: structured specs (product, quantity, material, sizes, delivery), evidence
from the ROOPAC catalogue, and a WhatsApp draft for the customer.

## Register

`product` - design serves the task. Merchandiser or sales staff at a desk, mid-enquiry,
needs a trustworthy brief fast. Warm paper + ink identity (a print company that lives
around paper), restrained accent, dense-but-calm typography.

## Surfaces

- `/` - brief builder: composer input, stage tracker, seven grounded result sections, copy actions, WhatsApp draft panel.
- `/health` - catalogue health findings.
- `/evals` - eval metrics and per-case table.

## Non-goals

Not a marketing site. No rebranding; the warm paper + ink token system is deliberate.
